# 10 — Kharadron Overlords: Sky-Ports and Subfaction Bonuses

Source: Champions of Order pp. 50–54 (faction lore, sky-port bonuses, Aetheric Navigator archetype).
Each entry gives the subfaction's **Subfaction Bonus**, marked in the Talents section of the sheet (Champions p. 19). Bonus text is condensed; mechanics are preserved.

## Faction overview (Champions p. 50)

- Species: Duardin. Faction: Kharadron Overlords.
- Governed by the **Kharadron Code**, set at the Conference of Madralta by the seven greatest sky-ports.
- **Geldraad**: ruling council of 18 seats, allocated by sky-port. Barak-Nar holds 6 and Barak-Zilfin 5 at the time of the book. No sky-port can reach a majority without one of the big two.
- Structure: Admiral's Council at the nexus, then guilds (AetherKhemists, Nav-League, Endrineers, Fleet Service, and others), then Arkanaut Companies. Grundstok Corporation is a chartered company with political clout.
- Advancement is meritocratic in principle: Arkanaut Academies, annual Musterpress, promotion to captain, admiral, Geldraad.
- Aether-gold is the lifeblood of the sky-ports.

## Sky-port subfactions

| Subfaction | Page | Bonus |
|---|---|---|
| **Barak-Nar** | p. 51 | **The Steering Hand.** When commanding a subordinate or speaking to a large group (council, protest), double the dice gained from Training in Guile and Intimidation. The *Stirring Voice* Talent (p. 80) is added to your Archetype's Talent list. |
| **Barak-Zilfin** | p. 51 | **Windmaster.** If your aether-rig has an aether-endrin, its speed is Fast. If it does not, you may swap a part of your rig with the same power consumption for an aether-endrin instead. Gain an additional 1d6 to Awareness and Survival Tests when exploring an area for the first time. |
| **Barak-Zon** | p. 52 | **For the Ironstar.** When Attacking a Champion or Chosen type enemy, your Melee and Accuracy increase one step. |
| **Barak-Urbaz** | p. 52 | **Child of The Market City.** When you undertake the *Aethercraft*, *Endrineering* or *Shopping* Endeavours, the cost of equipment or materials is reduced by 50%, to a maximum saving of 500D. When you take *Run a Business* (p. 121), earn 4d6 × 10D instead of 2d6 × 10D. |
| **Barak-Mhornar** | p. 53 | **Who Strikes First, Strikes Hardest.** Gain additional dice equal to your Training in Guile on your first attack in the first round of combat. If that attack is made during a surprise round, gain additional dice equal to twice your Training in Guile instead. The *Criminal* Talent (p. 84) is added to your Archetype's Talent list. |
| **Barak-Thryng** | p. 53 | **Chronicle of Grudges.** If attacked or wronged by a creature, you may add its name to your chronicle (create a name if you do not know it). Against a creature in your chronicle, Melee and Accuracy increase one step, but you have Disadvantage on all social Tests made to interact with it. |

### Sky-port notes
- Barak-Nar and Barak-Zilfin hold the most Geldraad seats (6 and 5). Notes on seat counts are as of the book and may change.
- Barak-Mhornar's location is secret in the book. It has relocated to a sky-island chain behind a realmgate. Treat the location as a GM decision.

## Other Kharadron archetype

### Aetheric Navigator (Champions p. 54)
- Species: Duardin (Kharadron Overlords). Faction: Kharadron Overlords.
- Core Skill: **Survival**. Core Talent: **Nav-League Member**.
- Skills (7 XP): Arcana, Awareness, Ballistic Skill, Crafting, Determination, Guile, Intuition, Lore, Nature, Survival.
- Talents (choose 3): Acute Sense, Aethermatics Over Superstition, Backup Plan, Expert Coordinator, Observant, Scholar.
- Equipment: Aetheric Navigator's Rig (p. 110), a collection of maps and charts (p. 107), 85 drops of Aqua Ghyranis.
- Attributes: the Body / Mind / Soul values are in the printed stat block on p. 54. The text extraction does not reliably pair the numbers with their labels, so read them from the page.
