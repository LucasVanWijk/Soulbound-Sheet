# 10 — Stormcast Eternals and Sylvaneth: Stormhosts, Glades and Subfaction Bonuses

Source: Champions of Order pp. 55–67 (faction intros, Stormhost and glade bonuses, Knight-Zephyros archetype, Reforging Flaw table).
Each entry gives the subfaction's **Subfaction Bonus**, marked in the Talents section of the sheet (Champions p. 19). Bonus text is condensed; mechanics are preserved.

## Stormcast Eternals

### Faction structure (Champions p. 55)
- A Stormhost (typically 5,000–10,000 Stormcast) is divided into **Chambers** (most are Strike Chambers of about 300). Tempest Chambers include Sacrosanct (wizards), Extremis (shock cavalry) and Vanguard Auxiliary (rangers).
- Each Stormcast is assigned a **Conclave** (combat specialism) and a **Retinue** (3–20 warriors).
- **Lords of the Storm** have no Conclave or Retinue. Most Chambers are led by a **lord-celestant**.
- **Knights of the realms** are junior officers or support staff assigned individual missions. They are not given a Retinue.
- Stormcast can be seconded to Soulbound. Rarely they are recruited from another Stormhost, and those chosen are usually outcasts.

### Reforging (Champions pp. 56–57)
- Every Stormcast has been Reforged at least once. Later Reforgings carry greater cost.
- Reforging can take a week to years. The player may need a new character or to control an NPC while waiting.
- **Humanity Lost:** each Reforging can give a new Reforging Flaw (roll on the table below), or the GM can worsen an existing flaw.
- **Death in All But Name:** Stormcast may suffer Final Death, become a lightning gheist, or be too damaged to Reforge.

### Knight-Zephyros archetype (Champions p. 62)
- Species: Stormcast Eternal. Core Skill: **Weapon Skill and Stealth**. Core Talent: **Lightning-fast Strikes**.
- Skills (5 XP): Awareness, Ballistic Skill, Guile, Intuition, Reflexes, Stealth, Survival, Weapon Skill.
- Talents (choose 3): Ambidextrous, Backstab, Criminal, Dirty Fighting*, Opportunist, Relentless Assault.
- Equipment: Sigmarite plate (Medium Armour), two whirlwind axes (2 Handaxes), a boltstorm pistol (Repeater Pistol), a wolf-pelt cloak, 150 drops of Aqua Ghyranis.
- Attributes: the Body / Mind / Soul values are in the printed stat block on p. 62. The text extraction does not reliably pair the numbers with their labels, so read them from the page.

## Sylvaneth

### Faction structure (Champions p. 63)
- The **Everqueen Alarielle** is at the top, advised by the **Regents of the Royal Moot**. Each glade is ruled by a Treelord Ancient.
- Glades divide into **clans**, which emerge from birth groupings in **soulpod groves**.
- Newborns are **Forest Folk** (most common), **Noble Spirits** (disciplined leaders and warriors who form each clan's Household), or **Free Spirits** (outside clan boundaries, pledged to the Everqueen alone; e.g. Kurnoth Hunters).
- **Revenants** appear only in war. **Outcasts** are the most malicious Sylvaneth and are not sanctioned.
- Lamentiri hold the memories of dead Sylvaneth. Some glades' Soulbound lose their lamentiri to the Binding (Gnarlroot and Harvestboon, per their entries in the book).

### Validation notes
- Oakenbrow, Harvestboon, Ironbark and Winterleaf reference Doom and Soulfire. Check those values in `01-core-rules/mettle-soulfire-doom.md`.
- Dreadwood's Doom bonus is a **permanent minimum** of 2. Enforce it in the Doom tracker.
- Winterleaf's bonus applies only while Soulfire is below the current Doom.
- Ironbark's Natural Armour changes the base 2. The character sheet's armour total must reflect it.
