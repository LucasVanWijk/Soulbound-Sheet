# Species

Source: Soulbound Corebook, Chapter 2 (Character Creation), pp. 20–27, plus species references in Chapter 3 (Archetypes).

Each character has a **Species**. The chosen **Archetype** determines the Species or offers a choice of Species (see `04-archetypes/`). Each Species grants a unique **Species Bonus**, applied **after** Attributes, Skills and Talents have been determined.

The corebook presents five Species: **Human, Stormcast Eternal, Aelf, Duardin, Sylvaneth**. Other Species (Orruk, Skaven, Vampire, etc.) are not playable in the core book; guidance for creating custom Species is on p. 296.

---

## Human

- **Species Bonus: Adaptable.** You gain **one additional Talent**. It may be any Talent you meet the requirements for (from Skills and Talents chapter).
- **Restriction:** If you have the *Spellcasting* Talent, you cannot use the Human Species Bonus to take that Talent again.
- Factions available to Humans (Archetype references): Collegiate Arcane (p. 187), Devoted of Sigmar (p. 190).

## Stormcast Eternal

- **Species is treated as distinct from Human** in game terms.
- **Restriction:** Stormcast Eternals are bound to Sigmar. They **cannot become Soulbound** (cannot be part of a Binding as a Soulbound — the rules say they "can never become part of the Binding") and **cannot use Soulfire**.
- **Species Bonus: Reforged.**
  - During character creation, roll **1d6** to determine how many times you have been Reforged. If you roll a **6**, roll again and add the results together. Continue for any further 6s.
  - When you die, you **may be Reforged**. Work with the GM on a narratively appropriate return time (hours to years).
- Starting XP: Stormcast Eternals begin **40 XP** in freeform creation (instead of 35) and their equipment has the **Sigmarite** Trait (freeform rule, p. 19).
- Stature: up to ~9 ft; no mechanical effect beyond the above.
- Optional roleplay: a "new" Stormcast may be agreed with the GM (no Reforge count).
- Factions available (Archetype references): Stormcast Eternals archetypes (Knight-Azyros, Knight-Incantor, Knight-Questor, Knight-Venator), see `04-archetypes/`.

## Aelf

- **Species Bonus: Long-Lived.** You gain an **additional 2 XP** to spend on **Skills** during character creation. **This XP must be spent on the same Skill.**
- Aelves begin with 2 additional XP to spend on Skills (p. 27 note; consistent with Long-Lived bonus).
- Factions available: Darkling Covens (p. 188), Daughters of Khaine (p. 189), Idoneth Deepkin (p. 193), Scourge Privateers (p. 195).

## Duardin

- **Species Bonus: Too Stubborn to Die.** Your **maximum Wounds increases by 2**, giving you **two additional spaces** on your Wound Track (see Wounds, p. 39 / Rules `01-core-rules`).
- Factions available: Fyreslayers (p. 192), Kharadron Overlords (p. 194).
- Kharadron: when creating a Kharadron character in the freeform method, choose one of the sample rigs on p. 116 (see `09-equipment`).
- Fyreslayers: in freeform creation, choose a number of **ur-gold runes equal to your Soul** (see p. 117, `09-equipment`).

## Sylvaneth

- **Species Bonus: Natural Armour.** Your **base Armour is 2**.
  - If your natural Armour is damaged, it **regrows and is repaired after a Rest**.
  - You **cannot wear armour**, but your natural Armour can be **reinforced** (see *Ironbark Oil*, p. 109).
- Factions available: Branchwych, Kurnoth Hunter, Tree-Revenant, Waypiper (Sylvaneth archetypes, p. 74–77).
- **Spirit-Song:** Sylvaneth can communicate non-verbally with other Sylvaneth in the Realm of Life by making a **Soul Test**. The GM sets the Difficulty based on distance and other factors. **No Skills are used** for this Test.

## Species Summary Table

| Species | Species Bonus | Mechanical Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Human | Adaptable | +1 Talent (any you qualify for; not a second *Spellcasting*) |
| Stormcast Eternal | Reforged | Roll 1d6 for Reforge count (6 = roll again and add); may be Reforged on death |
| Aelf | Long-Lived | +2 XP for Skills, must be spent on the **same** Skill |
| Duardin | Too Stubborn to Die | +2 maximum Wounds (two extra Wound Track spaces) |
| Sylvaneth | Natural Armour | Base Armour 2; cannot wear armour; regrows after Rest |

## Species Restrictions (for validation)

- Stormcast Eternal: cannot be Soulbound; cannot use Soulfire.
- Human: Adaptable cannot be used to take *Spellcasting* a second time.
- Sylvaneth: cannot wear armour (natural Armour only).
