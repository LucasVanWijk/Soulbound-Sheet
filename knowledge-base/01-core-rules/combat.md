# Combat

## Flow of battle

- Combat is in **rounds** (about 6 seconds of in-game time).
- Each participant takes a turn in **Initiative** order (highest first).
- **Initiative** (derived, see `derived-stats.md`): Mind + Awareness Training + Reflexes Training.

### Ties (p. 135)
- Two player characters: decide at start of combat, keep that order for the battle.
- Player character vs enemy: player character acts first.
- Two NPCs: GM decides at start, keeps that order.
- Or: opposed Mind (Awareness) Test to decide (optional, p. 298).

### Surprise (p. 135)
- A participant who has the element of surprise gets a **surprise round** where only they act, in Initiative order among any surprised participants.
- Surprise round: **one Action only**. You may **not** spend Mettle for extra Actions or to double Training or Focus. Mettle may still fuel Talents or abilities used as part of the Action.

## Your turn (p. 135–136)

On your turn you may **Move** and take **one Action**. Move may be taken before or after the Action, or split (Move, Action, Move).

- **Free Actions** (no Action cost): draw a weapon, reload a gun that does not have the Reload trait, open a door, drink an elixir, move within your Zone, fall prone willingly.
- Extra Actions can be bought with Mettle (see `mettle-soulfire-doom.md`).

## Zones (p. 136–137)

- Battlefields are split into **Zones** along natural divisions (walls, doors, trees). They have no fixed size.
- Most battlefields have at least 2 Zones. Zones can be created or destroyed in play.
- Flying combatants may use the sky as Zones.
- **Environmental Traits** affect an entire Zone.

## Ranges (p. 139)

| Range | Meaning | Movement |
|---|---|---|
| **Close** | Arm's reach. Melee attacks, manipulating objects, inspecting | — |
| **Short** | Within your current Zone (about 15–30 ft) | Free Action to move here |
| **Medium** | Any adjacent Zone (about 50–60 ft) | Requires Move |
| **Long** | Two Zones away (100 ft or more) | Requires Move + Run Action |
| **Extreme** | 3 or more Zones away. Narrative only | — |

Moving between Zones has no penalty for leaving an enemy's reach, but some enemies and Talents (Opportunist, page 88) can react to it.

## Speed (p. 140)

| Speed | In current Zone (Short) | To adjacent Zone (Medium) | Two Zones (Long) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Normal** | Free Action | Move | — |
| **Fast** | Free Action | Move | Move (up to two Zones) |
| **Slow** | Move | Run Action | — |

## Difficult terrain (p. 137–138)

- Beginning your turn in difficult terrain reduces Speed one step, to a minimum of **Slow**.
- Moving into difficult terrain uses your remaining movement. Moving further requires the Run Action.
- **Body (Reflexes) Tests** are **+1 Difficulty** while in difficult terrain.
- Talent *The Bigger They Are* (page 91) is an exception.

## Climbing, crawling, swimming, squeezing (p. 140)

- Speed is reduced one step, to a minimum of **Slow**.
- GM may require a Test (e.g. Body (Athletics) to climb a slippery cliff).

## Prone (p. 140)

- Falling prone willingly = Free Action.
- Standing from prone = Move.
- Moving while prone = crawl (uses the reduced rules above).

## Falling (p. 140)

- **1 Damage per 10 feet** fallen, and knocked **Prone**.
- Short controlled falls (up to 30 ft): a successful Body (Athletics) or Body (Reflexes) Test reduces Damage by the number of successes.

## Jumping (p. 141)

- Standing vertical leap: up to **Body** feet. Doubled with a short run-up.
- Standing long jump: **Body × 2** feet. Doubled with a short run-up.
- Beyond normal reach: GM may ask for Body (Athletics).

## Mounts (p. 141)

- Mount must be willing, **one size larger** than you, with the appropriate anatomy.
- Mounting uses your Move. Dismounting is a Free Action unless secured (then an Action).
- When you Move while mounted, use the **mount's Speed**.
- Moving the mount against its will: Body (Reflexes) Test or fall Prone within Close Range. Secured = automatic success.
- Mount knocked Prone: Body (Reflexes) Test to leap clear within Short Range. Failure = Prone. Secured = automatic failure. A flying mount knocked prone → you both fall.
- Attackers may target you or your mount.
- Controlled/trained mount acts on your Initiative; intelligent or untrained mount acts on its own.

## Flying (p. 141)

- Flight uses the Speed of the mount or equipment (e.g. Aether-endrin Fly (Normal), Celestial Wings).
- If the equipment is removed or inoperable, or the mount is unseated, you fall.

## Vehicles (p. 141–142)

- No rules for large vehicles; they are locations that move. Personal vehicles (e.g. gyrocopter) use the Mounts rules.

## Cover (p. 137)

| Type | Condition | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Partial Cover** | At least half of body blocked | Defence **+1 step** vs ranged attacks. Creatures hiding in Partial Cover have Advantage to avoid detection |
| **Total Cover** | Most or all of body blocked | Defence **+2 steps** vs ranged attacks. Creatures hiding have **Greater Advantage** on Opposed Tests to remain unseen |

A target benefits from only one type of cover at a time.

## Obscured (p. 139)

- **Lightly Obscured** (mist, shadow, faint light): Difficulty of sight-based Mind (Awareness) Tests **+1**. Opposed Mind (Awareness) Tests are at **Disadvantage**.
- **Heavily Obscured** (thick smoke, complete darkness): creatures in the Zone are **Blinded**.

## Hazards (p. 138)

| Hazard | Examples | Damage on entering or starting turn there |
|---|---|---|
| **Minor** | thorny branches, small fire | 1 |
| **Major** | poisonous spores, boneshard hail | 3 |
| **Deadly** | boiling lava, acidic silver rivers | 5 |

Interactive objects (doors, valves, torches) can create Hazards or Obscured Zones.

## Size (p. 138)

| Size | Height/Length | Example | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tiny | up to 2 ft | Mouse, Scryfish | — |
| Small | up to 4 ft | Grot, Bittergrub | — |
| Medium | up to 8 ft | Aelf, Duardin, Human, Stormcast Eternal | — |
| Large | up to 15 ft | Kurnoth Hunter, Gryph-charger, Rat Ogor | Restraining weapons affect Large or smaller |
| Enormous | up to 30 ft | Maw-Krusha, Magmadroth | Occupies an entire Zone; enemies cannot end turn in its Zone unless mounted/climbing; can melee into adjacent Zones |
| Monstrous | 30 ft+ | Harkraken, Megalofin | As Enormous, but occupies two or more Zones |

Size otherwise has no mechanical effect except in roleplay.

## Attacking (p. 145–146)

### The Ladder (p. 145)

Ratings: **Poor → Average → Good → Great → Superb → Extraordinary**. Each step is one rating.

### Attack DN (p. 146)

Compare attacker's **Melee** (melee) or **Accuracy** (ranged) with target's **Defence**:

| Attacker vs Defence | DN |
|---|---|
| 2+ steps higher | 2 |
| 1 step higher | 3 |
| Equal | 4 |
| 1 step lower | 5 |
| 2+ steps lower | 6 |

Attack Tests have no Complexity; successes add to Damage.

### Melee attack (p. 146)
1. Choose a target within **Close Range**.
2. Compare Melee with target's Defence → DN.
3. Make a **Body (Weapon Skill)** Test.
4. Success → deal Damage (see `damage-and-healing.md`).

### Ranged attack (p. 147)
1. Choose a target within the weapon's range.
2. Compare **Accuracy** with target's Defence → DN.
3. Make a **Body (Ballistic Skill)** Test.
4. Success → deal Damage.

**Range penalties (p. 147):**
- Target **one range beyond** weapon's range → Accuracy **−1 step**.
- Target **two ranges beyond** → Accuracy **−2 steps**.
- Ranged attack at **Close Range** with a weapon that lacks the Close trait → Accuracy **−1 step** (Point Blank Talent removes this).
- Using a ranged weapon as a melee improvised weapon counts as Ineffective.

**Throwing non-Thrown items (p. 148):** Accuracy −1 step (Short), −2 (Medium), −3 (Long).

### Hidden attacks (p. 146)
Attacking an unaware enemy: their Defence is treated as **Poor** for that attack. It returns to normal after you reveal yourself.

### Dual wielding (p. 148–150)
- Attacking with a weapon in each hand counts as a **single attack** for bonuses (extra dice, Focus, Mettle).
- **Two melee weapons:** split the dice pool between two Close Range targets. Compare Melee with each Defence. Focus in Weapon Skill can be applied once, to any die.
- **Two ranged weapons:** split the pool between two targets within each weapon's range. Compare Accuracy with each Defence. Focus in Ballistic Skill applied once.
- **Melee + ranged:** create pool from **Body only**; split it. Primary weapon gets extra dice for Training in that weapon, secondary gets none. Compare Melee for the melee target and Accuracy for the ranged target. Focus applies only to the primary weapon's dice.
- Talents *Ambidextrous* (adds a die when attacking with two weapons), *Relentless Assault* (Melee bonus with two melee weapons), *Gunslinger* (Accuracy bonus with two ranged weapons) — see `../06-talents/`.

### Damage steps (p. 150)
1. The successful attack deals Damage (weapon damage + successes).
2. Target reduces Toughness by **Damage minus Armour**.
3. If Toughness reaches 0, remaining Damage becomes Wounds (see `damage-and-healing.md`).

## Actions (p. 142–145)

| Action | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Attack** | Make an attack test (see Attacking above) |
| **Charge** | Move as far as Speed allows, then attack. Cannot charge an enemy in your own Zone. **+1d6** to the attack pool. **Defence −1 step** until start of your next turn. Mounted: both you and your mount lose a step |
| **Called Shot** | Target's Defence increases by hit location. **Head** +2 steps (on Damage: Stunned until end of its next turn). **Arms** +1 step (on Damage: drops held item). **Legs** +1 step (on Damage: Prone). If Defence cannot increase (already two steps higher), GM may disallow |
| **Defend** | Choose an ally within Close Range. Attacks and spells targeting them target you instead until start of your next turn, and you use your Defence and resist spells. Or defend a Zone: enemy entering must use an Action for Body (Might or Reflexes) Test opposed by your Body (Might); with a shield you have Advantage. Failure = barred. Help may add dice |
| **Dodge** | Until start of your next turn: **Defence +1 step** and **+1d6** to Body (Reflexes) Tests |
| **Flee** | Declare at start of turn. Spend Action, Move **and all current Mettle**. You leave combat, are removed from Initiative, and **Doom +1** |
| **Grapple** | Body (Might) Opposed Test vs target's Body (Might) or Body (Reflexes). Success: target **Restrained**. You may release at any time as a Free Action. Target may escape on its turn by repeating the Opposed Test |
| **Help** | Assist an ally: their next Test gets **+1d6 + 1d6 per your Training** in the Skill. Or help an ally attack an enemy within Close Range: their next attack gets **+1d6 + 1d6 per your Weapon Skill Training** (applies even to ranged attacks) |
| **Hide** | Body (Stealth) Test, modified by light, cover, weather. Hidden from any creature whose Natural Awareness is lower than your successes |
| **Improvise** | Describe anything; GM decides if possible and whether a Test is needed |
| **Parley** | Opposed Test set by GM (e.g. Body (Intimidation) vs Soul (Determination), Mind (Guile) vs Mind (Intuition)). Persuasion is not mind control |
| **React** | Declare trigger and response. If the trigger does not happen this turn, you may pass to next round without an Action, or take a different Action and act last in Initiative for the rest of combat |
| **Retreat** | Your turn ends. Each ally at start of their turn is asked if they retreat. If all agree, party escapes (GM may jump ahead or call a Test). If any member refuses, you and any ally who chose to retreat may remain or leave. **Doom +1** regardless |
| **Run** | Move to an adjacent Zone (Medium Range). Often combined with Move to reach two Zones away |
| **Search** | Mind (Awareness) Test, DN set by GM. May also use Soul (Awareness) or Mind (Intuition) to sense magic or leylines |
| **Seize the Initiative** | Do not act this turn; go to the top of Initiative at start of next round until someone else seizes it |
| **Shove** | Body (Might) Opposed Test vs Body (Might) or Body (Reflexes). Success: target no longer at Close Range |
| **Use an Object** | For objects needing more than a normal interaction (GM decides) |
| **Use a Talent** | Use a Talent. Mettle costs are spent with this Action |
| **Cheat Death** | Soulfire Action, see `mettle-soulfire-doom.md` |
| **Recover Toughness** | Soulfire Action, see `mettle-soulfire-doom.md` |

## Conditions

Full definitions from the corebook, p. 342.

| Condition | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Blinded** | The creature cannot see. Difficulty of Mind (Awareness) Tests that rely on sight is increased by 2. Opposed Mind (Awareness) Tests are made at Greater Disadvantage. Melee, Accuracy and Defence decrease one step. |
| **Charmed** | The creature cannot attack the source of the charm, and cannot target it with abilities or spells that would affect it negatively. The charmer has Advantage in social interactions with the target, and the target has Disadvantage. |
| **Deafened** | The creature cannot hear. The dice pool for any Test that requires hearing is reduced by 1. |
| **Frightened** | While the source of the fear is in line of sight, the creature rolls one less die for all Tests. It cannot willingly move closer to the source of its fear. |
| **Incapacitated** | The creature can't Move, take Actions, or spend Mettle. It can't defend itself: the DN to hit it is always 2. |
| **Poisoned** | Dice pool reduced by 1 for all Tests. |
| **Prone** | The creature can only move by crawling (p. 140), unless it uses its Move to stand up and end the Condition. Melee and Accuracy decrease one step. A creature attacking it from Close Range increases its Melee or Accuracy one step. A creature attacking from outside Close Range decreases its Accuracy one step per Zone: one step for Short Range, two for Medium, three for Long. |
| **Restrained** | The creature cannot move. Melee, Accuracy and Defence decrease one step. |
| **Stunned** | The creature can take either an Action or a Move, but not both. It cannot spend Mettle to take additional Actions. Speed becomes Slow (p. 140). Defence decreases one step. |
| **Unconscious** | The creature immediately drops anything it is holding, falls Prone, and is Incapacitated. It can't move or speak, and is unaware of its surroundings. |

### Where conditions come from (rules in this knowledge base)
- **Stunned:** Mortally Wounded (removed by a successful Death Test or Last Stand, see `damage-and-healing.md`). Called Shot to the Head.
- **Prone:** falling, Called Shot (Legs), mounts, Roar of Grungni, Skyhook results. Standing requires Move.
- **Restrained:** Grapple, restraining weapons, manacles, grapnel launcher.
- **Poisoned:** poison (one day), Roar of Grungni (until the start of your next turn).
- **Blinded:** Heavily Obscured Zones (see Zones above).
- **Frightened:** Mor'phann subfaction bonus (`10-subfactions/README.md`), other Frightening effects.
- **Mortally Wounded:** not a listed Condition here. See `damage-and-healing.md`.
