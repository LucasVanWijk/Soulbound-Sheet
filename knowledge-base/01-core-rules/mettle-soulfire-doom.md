# Mettle, Soulfire & Doom

Source: corebook pp. 129–134.

Three resources:

| Resource | Owner | Where tracked | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mettle** | Each character | Character sheet | Extra Actions, Talent and Miracle costs, Double Training/Focus (combat) |
| **Soulfire** | The Binding (shared, players) | Party sheet | Powerful shared effects. Only Binding members can use it |
| **Doom** | The party (GM uses it) | Party sheet | Gauge of the world's peril. Not spent |

## Mettle (p. 129–131)

### Maximum and recovery

- **Maximum Mettle = Soul ÷ 2, rounded up** (see `derived-stats.md`).
- Recover **1 Mettle at the start of your turn**, up to the maximum.
- Talents and spells can raise or lower the maximum.
- Outside combat, Mettle is not usually tracked: you regain 1 per turn, so you are back to full in seconds. Mettle used to heal Toughness can be used freely outside combat.

### Uses

| Use | Cost | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Take an extra Action** | 1 Mettle per Action | On your turn you may take another Action from the Actions list |
| **Use a Talent or Miracle** | Listed on the Talent or Miracle | Spent when you take the *Use a Talent* Action. Some (e.g. *Unbind*) can be triggered on another creature's turn |
| **Run** | 1 Mettle (as an extra Action) | Example: Move + Action + Mettle to reach the next Zone |
| **Double your Training** | 1 Mettle | When making a combat Test (attack, spell), double the dice gained from Training in that Skill. Only in combat |
| **Double your Focus** | 1 Mettle | After a combat Test, double the bonus from Focus in that Skill. Only in combat |

Restrictions:
- In a **surprise round**, you cannot take extra Actions and cannot double Training or Focus. You can spend Mettle for Talents or abilities used in your single Action.
- Flee spends all current Mettle (see `combat.md`).
- Last Stand refills Mettle to maximum (see `damage-and-healing.md`).
- Soulfire can refill Mettle (see below).

### Examples (p. 130)
- Vel (Body 4, Training 2 in Weapon Skill) doubles 2d6 to 4d6 for a total pool of 8d6 against armoured Blood Warrior.
- Double Focus (2) after an attack: apply +4 across dice, turning misses into successes.

## Soulfire (p. 131–133)

### Who can use it

- Only members of the **Binding** (the party of Soulbound) can spend Soulfire.
- **Stormcast Eternals cannot use Soulfire**, though they still count as party members for Last Stand refills.

### Spending Soulfire

1. Declare that you are spending Soulfire and how much.
2. The **rest of the Binding must agree**.
3. If they refuse: assent, or spend anyway and **Doom +1** (you used their strength without consent).
   - Groups may disable this rule and always require consent.

### Effects

| Effect | Cost | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Maximise successes** | 1 Soulfire | Before rolling, count every die as a 6. Cannot be combined with Double Training |
| **Reroll dice** | 1 Soulfire | Reroll any or all dice used in the Test, including bonus dice from spells and Talents |
| **Regain all Mettle** | 1 Soulfire | Immediately regain all spent Mettle, up to maximum |
| **Recover Toughness** | 1 Soulfire (Action) | Recover all Toughness |
| **Cheat Death** | 1 Soulfire (Action) | Target a Mortally Wounded Binding member within Close Range (may be yourself). They are no longer Mortally Wounded and recover **half** total Toughness. Retain existing Wounds |

Other effects may exist in special circumstances, artefacts or magical locations.

### Tracking Soulfire (p. 133)

- **Maximum Soulfire = number of party members (player characters) − any Stormcast Eternal characters.**
- Leaving the Binding reduces the max; joining raises it.
- Current and maximum are tracked on the party sheet (tokens recommended).

### Recovering Soulfire (p. 133)

| Trigger | Result |
|---|---|
| Binding achieves a **Short-term Party Goal** | Recover 1 |
| Binding achieves a **Long-term Party Goal** | Recover **all** |
| Maximum Soulfire **increases** | Recover **all** |
| A member takes **Recuperate** Endeavour | Recover 1 |
| Binding takes **Bond** Endeavour | Recover **all** |
| A party member makes a **Last Stand** | Refill to new maximum (Stormcast Last Stand refills too) |
| GM discretion: acts of kindness, nobility or loyalty | Recover some or all |

### Increasing maximum Soulfire (p. 133)

- Usually equals party size minus Stormcast. Raised by GM when the party performs a great deed that improves lives in the Mortal Realms (banishing Chaos from a besieged city, securing a trade route, reclaiming an Age of Myth artefact).
- Increasing maximum Soulfire also decreases Doom by 1 (see below).

### Leaving a Binding
- Possible but rare. See p. 34. New characters joining: see p. 289.

## Doom (p. 133–135)

- **Starts at 1. Never below 1.**
- Tracked on the party sheet and used by the GM.

### Uses
1. **World description:** low Doom = people go about their lives; higher Doom = bickering, then violence; high Doom = people on a knife-edge, theft, bloodshed. See p. 292.
2. **Mechanical effect:** as Doom grows, enemies may gain Armour, extra attacks or abilities. See p. 294 for rumours, fears and threats.

### Increasing Doom

| Trigger | Change |
|---|---|
| A party member **dies** | +1 |
| A Binding member uses **Soulfire without consent** | +1 |
| A party member **Flees** | +1 |
| The party **fails a Retreat** (or disharmony when a member refuses) | +1 |
| A **Fear escalates to a Threat** | +1 |
| GM judgement: party fails a task or allows a calamity (lost settlement, artefact falls to enemies) | +1 or more |

### Decreasing Doom

| Trigger | Change |
|---|---|
| Maximum Soulfire **increases** | −1 |
| Party takes **Cleanse Corruption** Endeavour (Binding) | −1 |
| A Stormcast Eternal party member is **Reforged** | −1 |
| The party **stops a Threat** | −1 |

Doom can never go below 1.
