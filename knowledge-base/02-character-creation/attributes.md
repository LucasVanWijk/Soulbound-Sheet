# Attributes (Character Creation Step 2)

Source: Corebook pp. 26–27.

## The Three Attributes

Each character has **three core Attributes**: **Body, Mind, Soul**.

| Attribute | Represents | Affects |
|---|---|---|
| **Body** | Physical strength, reflexes, coordination, body awareness | **Melee** (close combat effectiveness) and **Defence** (avoiding damage) |
| **Mind** | Intelligence, awareness, perception, deduction, thinking on your feet | **Accuracy** (ranged weapons), **Initiative** (combat reactions), **Natural Awareness** (inherent surroundings awareness) |
| **Soul** | Inner being, sense of self, spirit, determination, resisting Chaos | **Mettle** (effectiveness with Miracles, and extra actions in combat) |

## Attribute Rating

- Player characters have Attribute ratings from **1 to 8**; some powerful creatures exceed this.
- An average person has **1 or sometimes 2** in each Attribute.
- Shown in parentheses, e.g. **Body (3)**.
- A Test using an Attribute rolls **a number of d6 equal to the Attribute rating** (e.g. Body (4) → 4d6 for a Body Test).
- Attributes increase as you advance and **may decrease** after a grievous wound or trauma.

## Starting Attributes

- Determined by your **Archetype** (a typical example of that Archetype).
- **Modifying Attributes:** Before moving to Skills and Talents, you may **swap the values of two Attributes** (p. 27).
  - Example: Isharann Soulscryer (Body 1, Mind 2, Soul 4) swapped to Body 4, Mind 2, Soul 1 by swapping Body and Soul.

## Attribute Effects Quick Reference (derived stats, see `01-core-rules`)

- **Melee** = derived from Body
- **Defence** = derived from Body
- **Accuracy** = derived from Mind
- **Initiative** = derived from Mind
- **Natural Awareness** = derived from Mind
- **Mettle** = derived from Soul

> Note: the exact derivation formulas for Melee, Defence, Accuracy, Initiative, Natural Awareness and Mettle are listed in `01-core-rules/combat-and-derived-stats.md` once extracted.
