# Physical Appearance Tables (Optional Random Generation)

Source: Corebook pp. 30–32. Purely cosmetic. No mechanical effect. Useful for random character generation in the app.

## Age in Years

| Species | Formula |
|---|---|
| Human | 3d6 + 15 |
| Aelf | 6d6 × 5 |
| Duardin | 4d6 × 5 |
| Sylvaneth | 1d6 × 1d6 × 1d6 |
| Stormcast Eternal | Use the **Human** column for apparent age (actual age is GM/player choice) |

## Height

Format: base + roll.

| Species | Height |
|---|---|
| Human | 4'10" + 3d6" |
| Stormcast Eternal | 6'8" + 3d6" |
| Aelf | 5'10" + 2d6" |
| Duardin | 4'1" + 2d6" |
| Branchwych / Tree-Revenant (Sylvaneth) | 5'4" + 2d6" |
| Kurnoth Hunter (Sylvaneth) | (3 + 2d6)' + 1d6" |

Human rule: if any die shows a 6, roll 1d6 and add that many inches.

## Distinguishing Features

Roll 1d6 for the **column** and 1d6 for the **row**. Roll as many times as you like ("often less is more").

| 1d6 | Column 1: Scar | Column 2: Tattoo | Column 3: Piercing | Column 4: Scent | Column 5: Accent/Speech | Column 6: Affectation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pockmarked cheeks | Ocean waves along arms | Ear | Freshly cut grass | Slow and deliberate | Plays with hair/beard |
| 2 | Gash across left eye | Holy symbol on rump | Septum | Stale meat | Quick and precise | Flips a coin to make decisions |
| 3 | Burned hand | Intricate maze on back | Neck | Ocean mist | Thinks out loud | Wears two hats |
| 4 | Noose mark around throat | Strange arcane markings on chest | Eyebrow | Rotten eggs | Lazy drawl | Imitates a different Species |
| 5 | Missing ear | Broken chains on wrists | Tongue | Oil and grease | Rough whisper | Always snacking |
| 6 | Frequently broken nose | Kill count on forearm | Belly-button | Smoke and ash | Bellowing and commanding | Whistles or sings when nervous |
