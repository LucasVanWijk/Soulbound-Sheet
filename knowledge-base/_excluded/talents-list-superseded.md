# Talent Reference (Alphabetical)

Source: Corebook pp. 82–92. Format per entry: **Requirement** (if any) · **Repeatable** · **Effect**.

> Cost for every Talent: **2 XP** unless noted. Talents marked `*` in Archetypes have a requirement outside the Core Skill.

---

## A

**A Warm Meal**
- Requirement: Cooking utensils, ingredients (equipment).
- Effect: When you take a **Rest**, you may spend the Rest cooking a meal. At the end of the Rest, the **Binding recovers 1 Soulfire**.

**Acute Sense**
- Requirement: Training (1) in Awareness.
- Effect: Choose one sense (sight, sound, touch, taste, smell). When making an Awareness Test with that sense, **the dice gained from Training are doubled**. GM may allow extra detection tests (e.g. smelling poison).

**Aether-Khemists Guild Member**
- Requirement: Duardin (Kharadron Overlords), Training (1) **and** Focus (1) in Crafting.
- Effect: Can take the **Aethercraft Endeavour** (create aetheric devices; p. 156).

**Alley Cat**
- Requirement: none stated.
- Effect: **Advantage** on Opposed Tests to hide in a city or to search for someone. Always knows which streets to take in a city; **never becomes lost in a city**.

**Ambidextrous**
- Requirement: none stated.
- Effect: Equally skilled in either hand. When **dual wielding** and making an Attack, **add 1d6** to the total dice pool **before** splitting dice per weapon.

**Ancestral Memories**
- Requirement: Kurnoth Hunter (Archetype).
- Effect: When you complete a **Rest**, gain **Training (1) or Focus (1)** in one Skill of your choice, or increase existing Training/Focus in a Skill by 1 (max 3). The benefit is kept until you finish a Rest, when you may choose a new Skill or keep the same one.

**Animal Friend**
- Requirement: none stated.
- Effect: **Advantage** on Opposed Tests to command or earn the trust of an animal. Knows what an animal wants or what is stressing or harming it.

**Arcane Discipline**
- Requirement: Focus (1) in Channelling.
- Effect: When your spell targets a **Zone**, you may choose a number of targets equal to your **Focus in Channelling** to be **unaffected** by the spell.

## B

**Backstab**
- Requirement: Training (1) **or** Focus (1) in Stealth, **and** a **Subtle** weapon.
- Effect: If you Attack with a Subtle weapon and the target is **unaware** of you, the attack **deals double Damage and ignores Armour**.

**Barazakdum, the Doom-oath**
- Requirement: Doomseeker (Archetype).
- Effect: Whenever your **Toughness is at 0**, Damage from your attacks is **doubled**. Applied **before** subtracting the target's Armour.

**Battle Rage**
- Requirement: none stated.
- Effect: As a **Free Action**, reduce your **Defence** by a number of steps to increase your **Melee** by an equal number of steps. Lasts until the **start of your next turn**.

**Blessed (Choose)**
- Requirement: Training (1) **and** Focus (1) in **Devotion**.
- Effect: Choose the god who has shown you favour and **choose 1 Miracle** (see `08-miracles/`). Miracles are explained on p. 93.
- Note: Blessed grants access to the Miracles of the chosen god. Several Archetypes receive Blessed automatically (Excelsior Warpriest, Hag Priestess, Auric Runesmiter, Isharann Soulscryer, Knight-Azyros).

**Blood Frenzy**
- Requirement: Witch Aelf (Archetype).
- Effect: On your turn, if you make a **melee Attack and deal Damage**, your **Melee increases one step** for your **next** Attack. This stacks for each subsequent Attack that deals Damage. Returns to normal at the **end of your turn**. No effect vs **Undead** or creatures that do not bleed.

**Bulwark**
- Requirement: none stated.
- Effect: If **two or more enemies** are within **Close Range** at the **start of your turn**, your **Defence increases one step**. Lasts until the start of your next turn.

## C

**Combat Ready**
- Requirement: none stated.
- Effect: **+2 Initiative**, and you **can never be Surprised** in combat. **Repeatable** — each take adds another +2 to Initiative.

**Combat Repairs**
- Requirement: Training (1) in Crafting, **smith's tools or endrineer's tools**.
- Effect: Attempt to repair an ally's **Armour** in combat. DN depends on rarity/familiarity:
  - **DN 4:1** — familiar with the material, or Armour is Common
  - **DN 5:1** — unfamiliar with the material, or Armour is Rare
  - **DN 6:1** — unfamiliar **and** material is unique/magical (e.g. Sigmarite, Sylvaneth natural armour)
- Success: restore **1 Armour per success**, up to the Armour's original rating. Repairs are **temporary**, lasting until end of combat.

**Contortionist**
- Requirement: none stated.
- Effect: **Advantage** on Opposed Tests to escape a grasp or slip away (e.g. when grappled, p. 143). Can fit into unusually small or cramped spaces.

**Criminal**
- Requirement: none stated.
- Effect: **Advantage** on Opposed Tests to steal from someone, or to go unnoticed during illicit activity. Can easily find havens of crime (illegal fight clubs, black markets).

**Crushing Blow**
- Requirement: **Body (3)**, a **Crushing** weapon.
- Effect: If you roll a **6** on an Attack with a Crushing weapon vs a **Large or smaller** creature, the target is **Stunned** until the end of its next turn.

## D

**Demolitions Expert**
- Requirement: Focus (1) in Crafting.
- Effect: When you use an explosive or weapon with the **Blast** Trait, choose a number of targets equal to your **Focus in Crafting** to be **unaffected** by the blast.

**Diplomat**
- Requirement: none stated.
- Effect: **Advantage** on Opposed Tests to resolve conflict, gauge moods and intentions, and tell when people are lying. People are generally courteous to you.

## E

**Eidetic Memory**
- Requirement: none stated.
- Effect: Near-perfect recall of memories. You can recall the major elements of an event; recalling minutiae (faces, numbers, words) may require a Test. **Skill and DN are set by the GM.**

**Endrineers Guild Member**
- Requirement: Duardin (Kharadron Overlords), Training (1) **and** Focus (1) in Crafting.
- Effect: Can take the **Endrineering Endeavour** to forge aetheric devices (p. 156).

## F

**Fearless**
- Requirement: none stated.
- Effect: When you would become **Frightened** from failing a Test, or are magically influenced to act against your will, you may immediately make a **DN 6:1 Mind (Determination)** Test to resist.

**Forbidden Knowledge**
- Requirement: none stated.
- Effect: On selection, choose **Arcana, Lore, or Theology**. Tests with that Skill **double the dice from Training**. **If you fail** such a Test, **Doom increases by 1** (GM may add future plots). **Repeatable**, choosing a new Skill each time.

## G

**Graceful Landing**
- Requirement: none stated.
- Effect: After a fall, make a **Body (Athletics)** Test to avoid Damage. **Difficulty = 1 per 10 ft fallen, max 6** (e.g. 30 ft = 3). On success, reduce Damage by the number of successes.

**Gunslinger**
- Requirement: Training (1) **and** Focus (1) in Ballistic Skill.
- Effect: While wielding a ranged weapon **in each hand**, your **Accuracy increases one step**.

**Guts**
- Requirement: Training (1) in Fortitude.
- Effect: **+1 Toughness per level of Training in Fortitude**.

## H

**Hail of Doom**
- Requirement: Training (1) **or** Focus (1) in Ballistic Skill, a **ranged weapon**.
- Effect: Choose a **Zone** within your weapon's range; make a **DN 4:1 Body (Ballistic Skill)** Test. Each enemy in the Zone makes an **Opposed DN 4:1 Body (Reflexes)** Test. On failure, the target suffers Damage equal to the **difference in successes**. If the target wins, no damage.

**Hard to Kill**
- Requirement: none stated.
- Effect: The **first** time you fail a **Death Test** in combat, the DN does **not** increase. The next failed Test increases the DN as normal.

**Heavy Hitter**
- Requirement: none stated.
- Effect: Unarmed strikes deal **1 + S Damage**, gain the **Crushing** Trait, and **lose the Ineffective Trait**.

**Heroic Challenge**
- Requirement: **Knight-Questor** (Archetype).
- Effect: As an **Action**, choose a creature within **Medium Range**. If that creature attacks any target other than you, its **Melee and Accuracy decrease one step**. If it is a spellcaster, the **Complexity of its offensive spells** that do not target you or your Zone **increases by 1**. Your **Melee increases one step** when attacking the challenged target. Lasts until end of combat.

**Hit and Run**
- Requirement: none stated.
- Effect: **Once per turn**, if you deal Damage with a melee attack, you may move to an **adjacent Zone** as a **Free Action**.

**Hunter**
- Requirement: none stated.
- Effect: **Advantage** on Opposed Tests to track, find, trap or hunt creatures. Choose one terrain type — **Desert, Forest, Grassland, Mountain, Tainted Lands, Tundra** — where you can find a relatively secure place to rest and **cannot be Surprised** while resting.

## I

**Immense Strikes**
- Requirement: **Body (4)**, a **Two-handed** melee weapon.
- Effect: **+1 Damage** with melee weapons that have the **Two-handed** Trait.

**Immense Swing**
- Requirement: **Body (4)**, a **Two-handed** melee weapon.
- Effect: Two-handed melee weapons you wield gain the **Cleave** Trait.

**Intimidating Manner**
- Requirement: none stated.
- Effect: **Advantage** on Opposed Tests to threaten, intimidate, or start arguments. People tend to leave you alone, even where you don't belong.

**Iron Stomach**
- Requirement: none stated.
- Effect: **Double the dice from Training in Fortitude** for Tests to resist **poison and disease**.

**Iron Will**
- Requirement: Training (1) in **Determination**.
- Effect: **+1 Toughness per level of Training in Determination**.

## L

**Legendary Saga**
- Requirement: Duardin (Fyreslayer), Training (1) in Entertain.
- Effect: As an **Action**, make a **Soul (Entertain)** Test to chant one saga. Lasts until the **start of your next turn**. Choose one:
  - **A Saga of Condemnation (DN 6:1)**: Complexity of any spell cast by an enemy with **Long Range** increases by 1.
  - **A Saga of Enmity (DN 5:1)**: Any ally that starts its turn in your Zone and can hear you increases its **Melee and Accuracy one step** until start of its next turn.
  - **A Saga of Grimnir (DN 6:1)**: Any ally that starts its turn in your Zone and can hear you deals **additional Damage on melee attacks equal to your successes** on the Entertain Test.

**Loyal Companion (Choose)**
- Requirement: none stated (companion may have Species/culture restrictions).
- Effect: Gain a companion. Companion gains a **bonus to Toughness equal to your Soul**. On your turn, use an **Action** to direct the companion to **Move and take an Action** (attack or use a Trait). Companions have **no Initiative** and act only when instructed. If the companion dies, take the **Train Companion Endeavour** to bond with a new one. Stats in Bestiary (Chapter 13).
- Companion options (restrictions shown): **Bittergrub** (Sylvaneth), **Drillbill** (Kharadron), **Efreet**, **Fangmora Eel** (Idoneth Deepkin), **Gryph-hound** (Free Peoples or Stormcast Eternals), **Horse**, **Mustori**, **Quiverling** (Sylvaneth), **Scryfish** (Idoneth Deepkin), **Spirit Guardian**, **Star-eagle** (Stormcast Eternal).

## M

**Martial Memories**
- Requirement: Tree-Revenant (Archetype).
- Effect: At the **start of combat**, choose one:
  - **Memories of War**: **Melee increases one step** for the duration of combat.
  - **Memories of Defiance**: **Defence increases one step** for the duration of combat.
  - **Memories of Dawn**: You **act first every round** for the duration of combat (overrides other effects that allow a creature to act first).

**Master of Disguise**
- Requirement: **Disguise Kit** (equipment).
- Effect: **Advantage** on Opposed Tests to disguise yourself or impersonate. On taking this Talent, create an **alternate persona** (name, appearance, acquaintances). Build it using the **False Identity Endeavour** during downtime.

**Medic**
- Requirement: Training (1) **and** Focus (1) in Medicine; suitable tools and supplies.
- Effect: Instead of taking a **Rest**, spend the Rest treating **one ally's Wound**. You do **not** gain the Rest benefits. Treat **one Wound at a time**; succeed on a **Mind (Medicine)** Test:
  - **Severe Wound: DN 4:2**
  - **Deadly Wound: DN 4:3**
  - On success, the ally's Wound severity reduces **two steps** at the end of the Rest (Severe → healed; Deadly → Minor).
  - On failure, the Wounds do not heal and the ally does not gain Rest benefits.

**Mounted Combatant**
- Requirement: Training (1) **or** Focus (1) in Beast Handling.
- Effect: When mounted, your **Melee increases one step**.

## N

**Night Vision**
- Requirement: none stated.
- Effect: No **Disadvantage** on sight-based Tests in low light or darkness.

## O

**Observant**
- Requirement: none stated.
- Effect: **Advantage** on Opposed Tests to spot hidden enemies. **Natural Awareness +1**. You suffer **no Defence penalty** when attacked by a hidden creature (see Attacks While Hidden, p. 146).

**Opportunist**
- Requirement: none stated.
- Effect: **Once per round**, when an opponent attempts to move away from you, you may immediately make an **Attack**.

**Orientation**
- Requirement: none stated.
- Effect: Senses the thaumaturgical waves of the realms; always knows **coreward** (toward realm centre) and **edgeward** (toward realm edge). Cannot become lost except via powerful magic or divine intervention.

## P

**Patient Strike**
- Requirement: none stated.
- Effect: You may **delay your turn** and act **last** in Initiative. Your **Melee and Accuracy increase one step**. On your turn you may Move, take Actions, and spend Mettle as normal. At the beginning of the next round, you return to your original Initiative position.

**Pierce Armour**
- Requirement: Training (1) in Awareness, a **Piercing** weapon.
- Effect: Ignore **1 point of Armour** for every **6** rolled on an Attack with a Piercing weapon.

**Point Blank Range**
- Requirement: Training (1) **and** Focus (1) in Ballistic Skill.
- Effect: **No Accuracy penalty** when using a ranged weapon at **Close Range**.

**Potent Spells**
- Requirement: Training (1) **and** Focus (1) in Channelling.
- Effect: When a creature must make a Test to resist one of your spells, the **number of successes required increases by 1**.

## Q

**Quick Reload**
- Requirement: none stated.
- Effect: You **ignore the Reload Trait** on any weapon you use.

## R

**Relentless Assault**
- Requirement: Training (1) **and** Focus (1) in Weapon Skill.
- Effect: While wielding a melee weapon **in each hand**, your **Melee increases one step**.

**Rending Blow**
- Requirement: **Body (3)**, Focus (1) in Might, melee weapon.
- Effect: Instead of dealing Damage, you may **sunder the target's Armour** by your successes on the Attack, to a maximum of **1 + your Focus in Might**. (E.g. Focus 2 → up to 3.) The Armour is **permanently damaged** and must be repaired.

## S

**Savvy**
- Requirement: none stated.
- Effect: **Advantage** on Opposed Tests to bargain with merchants or broker contracts. If a bribe fails, the target is less likely to take offence or report you.

**Scholar**
- Requirement: none stated.
- Effect: Choose a subject of study. **Double the dice from Training in Lore** for Tests on that subject.

**Sense Ur-Gold**
- Requirement: Duardin (Fyreslayer).
- Effect: **DN 4:1 Soul (Awareness)** Test to detect ur-gold. Failure or no ur-gold within **1,000 ft** = nothing sensed. Success within 1,000 ft = general direction. Within **Medium Range** = location to within a few feet. Within **Short Range** = exact location.

**Sever**
- Requirement: Training (1) in Medicine, a **Slashing** weapon.
- Effect: On a **6** on an Attack with a Slashing weapon, the target is knocked **Prone**. For the rest of combat, any time it Moves it suffers **1 Damage** (ignores Armour).

**Shield Mastery**
- Requirement: Training (1) **and** Focus (1) in Weapon Skill; **Training (3) and Focus (3) in Reflexes**.
- Effect: While wielding a shield, your **Defence increases by one additional step**.

**Sigmar's Judgement**
- Requirement: Training (1) **and** Focus (1) in Theology.
- Effect: **Melee and Accuracy increase one step** when your target is a **Daemon** or servant of **Chaos**.

**Silver Tongue**
- Requirement: none stated.
- Effect: **Advantage** on Opposed Tests to persuade, deceive or seduce. People remember you fondly and greet you warmly.
- Note: The Darkling Sorceress archetype lists "Silver Tongued"; treat as **Silver Tongue**.

**Sleight of Hand**
- Requirement: none stated.
- Effect: **Advantage** on Opposed Tests to distract, misdirect, steal or hide small objects. Even after a thorough search, you can hide a **palm-sized object** on your person.

**Spellcasting (Choose)**
- Requirement: Training (1) **and** Focus (1) in Channelling.
- Effect: Choose **one Lore of Magic** (below). You learn **Arcane Bolt** and **Mystic Shield**, plus **4 more spells** from the Common Spell list and your Lore's spell list. See `07-spells/`.
- **Repeatable** for additional Lores with extra XP (see `07-spells/README.md` for the table).
- Lores available (all listed in `07-spells/`): **Amber**, **Amethyst**, **Bright**, **Celestial**, **Gold**, **Grey**, **Jade**, **Light**, **The Deeps** (requires Aelf (Idoneth Deepkin)).

**Star-fated Arrow**
- Requirement: Knight-Venator (Archetype).
- Effect: As an **Action**, choose a creature you can see within **Long Range**. The **next ranged Attack** you make against it **ignores Armour**.

**Strong Soul**
- Requirement: Blessed (any), Training (2) in Devotion.
- Effect: **Mettle = (Soul + Training in Devotion) ÷ 2**, rounded up (replaces the normal Mettle calculation).

## T

**Tactician**
- Requirement: none stated.
- Effect: As an **Action**, choose an ally within **Medium Range**. The ally may make an **Attack** against a creature of your choice (if able and armed). If it does, it adds bonus dice equal to **your Mind** to its dice pool. Once per round per creature.

**The Bigger They Are**
- Requirement: none stated (Archetype Core Talent for Black Ark Corsair).
- Effect: Your **Melee is one step higher** when fighting **Large or greater** creatures.

**Trader's Cache**
- Requirement: Trade Pioneer (Archetype).
- Effect: Replenish your cache when you **Rest in a city** or between adventures (p. 155). Make a **DN 4:1 Mind (Survival)** Test; the successes = cache size (max = Mind + Training in Survival). Expend successes from the cache to **add that many successes to any Test**. When bargaining, you may spend successes to say you have the item wanted; **Rare = 2 successes**, **Exotic = 3+** (GM decides).

## U

**Unbind**
- Requirement: Training (1) **and** Focus (1) in Channelling.
- Effect: When a creature you can see within **Long Range** casts a spell, you may **spend 1 Mettle** to attempt to unbind it. Make a **Mind (Channelling)** Test. **Difficulty = the spell's Difficulty**; **Complexity = the caster's total successes**. Success = spell has no effect. Failure = spell takes effect.

**Unbreakable Spells**
- Requirement: Spellcasting (any), Focus (1) in Channelling.
- Effect: When a creature tries to unbind or disrupt your spells, the **Complexity of that Test increases by 1 per Focus level** you have in Channelling.

**Underdog**
- Requirement: none stated.
- Effect: If **two or more enemies and no allies** are within **Close Range** at the start of your turn, your **Melee increases one step**. Lasts until the start of your next turn.

## V

**Vanish**
- Requirement: Focus (1) in Stealth.
- Effect: You can attempt to **hide even without cover or shadows**.

## W

**Weapon Weave**
- Requirement: Branchwych (Archetype).
- Effect: When you Rest, make a **Soul (Crafting)** Test to weave a weapon from roots and vines:
  - **DN 4:1** — Any Common **Subtle** melee weapon, or a holy symbol of Alarielle.
  - **DN 5:1** — Any Common one-handed weapon, or a Shield.
  - **DN 6:1** — Any Common two-handed weapon, or a Bow.
  - Between adventures, take the **Magical Weapon Weave Endeavour** to imbue the weapon with magic.
  - Note: Sylvaneth rarely gift such weapons; a Branchwych often inscribes a mark of gift on them.

**Witch-Sight**
- Requirement: none stated.
- Effect: Perceives the **magical energy** of the realms. At GM discretion, can track the flow of magic, map ley-lines, or find spells and artefacts. Can be blinding in magic-rich areas.

## Z

**Zharrgrim**
- Requirement: Duardin (Fyreslayer), **Blessed (Grimnir)**, **Sense Ur-Gold**.
- Effect: Can take the **Forge Ur-Gold Rune Endeavour** (p. 157).

---

## Coverage Note

This list covers every Talent described on corebook pp. 82–92. Talents referenced on Archetype lists are all present above. Miracles (granted by *Blessed* and *Strong Soul*) are in `08-miracles/`. The Endeavours referenced by some Talents (e.g. *Aethercraft Endeavour*, *Forge Ur-Gold Rune Endeavour*, *Train Companion Endeavour*) are in the Between Adventures chapter (pp. 155–157) and are not part of this file.
