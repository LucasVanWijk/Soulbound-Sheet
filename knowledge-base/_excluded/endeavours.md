# Endeavours (Between Adventures)

Source: corebook pp. 155–159.

## Downtime rules (p. 155)

- Default: **no more than a week** of downtime before characters are called again.
- You can complete **one Endeavour** per week of downtime. If you have more time, you can take **one additional Endeavour per week, up to three** total.
- At the end of downtime, you recover **all Wounds** (see `../01-core-rules/damage-and-healing.md`).

### Stormcast Eternals (p. 155)
- Must spend their **first Endeavour** of each downtime **returning to their Stormhost**.
- If killed in the previous adventure, the GM may allow them to be **Reforged** during the downtime, so they can rejoin the party at the start of the next adventure. Reforging reduces Doom by 1 (see `../01-core-rules/mettle-soulfire-doom.md`).

## Rules for Endeavour Tests (p. 156)

- If an Endeavour requires a Test, **you cannot spend Soulfire or Mettle** to alter the dice pool or result.
- If the Test fails, choose one:
  - **Keep Trying:** with another week of downtime, extend the Endeavour and make 3 more Tests, to a total of 3 weeks (9 Tests).
  - **Abandon Your Work:** discard the unfinished item. Sell the materials for **half** their purchase value.
  - **Finish It Later:** if you have a secure location (workshop, ally's ship), keep the progress and resume later with the successes already achieved.

### Creating new Endeavours (p. 159)

GMs and players may create new Endeavours. Keep them to an activity completed in one week in one location. Any Extended Test is limited to **3 Tests total**, using any combination of Attributes and Skills.
