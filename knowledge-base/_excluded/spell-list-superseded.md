# Spell List

Source: Corebook pp. 267–281 (Common Spells and the nine Lores of Magic). Extracted column-by-column; every entry is also available as structured data in `spells.json` (same folder).

Entry format: **DN** (Channelling Test difficulty) · **Target** · **Range** · **Duration** · **Test** (if the effect needs a Test) · **Overcast** (what extra successes can buy) · effect text.

Spells are learned through the **Spellcasting** Talent (`06-talents/talents-list.md`). On taking Spellcasting you learn **Arcane Bolt** and **Mystic Shield**, then choose **4 more spells** from the **Common Spell** list and the list for your chosen **Lore of Magic**. See `README.md` for the rules.
