# Sylvaneth Archetypes

Source: Corebook pp. 74–77. Faction page reference: Sylvaneth (p. 197). Species: **Sylvaneth**.

**Species rules (see `03-species/`):**
- Base **Armour 2** (natural bark). **Cannot wear armour**; natural Armour can be reinforced with **Ironbark Oil** (p. 109).
- Natural Armour regrows after a **Rest** if damaged.
- **Spirit-Song** communication uses a Soul Test (no Skills).

> Lore is omitted. Only the rules-relevant stat blocks are kept.

---

## Branchwych

- **Species:** Sylvaneth
- **Attributes:** Body 2 · Mind 4 · Soul 2
- **Skills (7 XP):** Awareness, Arcana, Beast Handling, **Channelling (Core)**, Crafting, Lore, Nature, Medicine, Theology, Weapon Skill
- **Core Talents:** Spellcasting (**Jade**), Unbind
- **Talents (Choose 2):** Animal Friend, Loyal Companion (Bittergrub), Medic\*, Weapon Weave, Witch-Sight
- **Equipment:** Greenwood scythe (Greatscythe); a fragment of cyclestone from Ghyran; **310 drops** Aqua Ghyranis.

## Kurnoth Hunter

- **Species:** Sylvaneth
- **Attributes:** Body 4 · Mind 3 · Soul 1
- **Skills (6 XP):** Awareness, **Ballistic Skill OR Weapon Skill (choose one as Core)**, Beast Handling, Fortitude, Might, Nature, Reflexes, Stealth, Weapon Skill. *(Book lists Ballistic Skill and Weapon Skill both in the skill list; the Core Skill is the one chosen.)*
- **Core Talent:** Ancestral Memories
- **Talents (Choose 2):** Animal Friend, Bulwark, Hail of Doom, Hunter, Loyal Companion (Quiverling)
- **Equipment:** Greatbow (Bow); choose a **Greatsword or Greatscythe**; **280 drops** Aqua Ghyranis.

## Tree-Revenant Waypiper

- **Species:** Sylvaneth
- **Attributes:** Body 4 · Mind 2 · Soul 2
- **Skills (7 XP):** Awareness, Athletics, Entertain, Fortitude, Nature, Intuition, Intimidation, Reflexes, Survival, **Weapon Skill (Core)**
- **Core Talent:** Martial Memories
- **Talents (Choose 3):** Acute Sense, Combat Ready, Fearless, Hunter, Observant, Opportunist
- **Equipment:** Any Common melee weapon; waypipes; **280 drops** Aqua Ghyranis.

---

### Rules-relevant notes
- Sylvaneth are **Soulbound** only with Alarielle's decision; no mechanical effect beyond the archetype.
- Kurnoth Hunter: the book lists the Core Skill as "Ballistic Skill or Weapon Skill". Validation should require exactly one of these as the Core Skill.
- The Kurnoth Hunter's Skill XP is **6** (not 7 or 9); this is the only Archetype with 6 Skill XP in the core book.
- Sylvaneth Species Bonus is applied after Attributes/Skills/Talents (see `03-species/`).
