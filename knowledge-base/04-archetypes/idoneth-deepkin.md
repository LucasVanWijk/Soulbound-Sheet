# Idoneth Deepkin Archetypes

Source: Corebook pp. 61–64. Faction page reference: Idoneth Deepkin (p. 193). Species: **Aelf (Idoneth Deepkin)**.

> Lore is omitted. Only the rules-relevant stat blocks are kept.

---

## Akhelian Emissary

- **Species:** Aelf (Idoneth Deepkin)
- **Attributes:** Body 4 · Mind 2 · Soul 1
- **Skills (7 XP):** Athletics, **Beast Handling (Core)**, Determination, Dexterity, Fortitude, Guile, Intimidation, Intuition, Reflexes, Weapon Skill
- **Core Talent:** Loyal Companion (Fangmora)
- **Talents (Choose 4):** Combat Ready, Diplomat, Fearless, Intimidating Manner, Mounted Combatant, Night Vision, Opportunist
- **Equipment:** Akhelian chestplate (Medium Armour); a helsabre (Sword) **and** shield, **or** a voltspear (Pike); ethersea cloak; a necklace with a shard of seashell from your enclave; **40 drops** Aqua Ghyranis.

## Isharann Soulscryer

- **Species:** Aelf (Idoneth Deepkin)
- **Attributes:** Body 1 · Mind 2 · Soul 4
- **Skills (7 XP):** Arcana, Awareness, Beast Handling, **Devotion (Core)**, Intuition, Lore, Nature, Survival, Theology, Weapon Skill
- **Core Talent:** Blessed (The Ethersea)
- **Talents (Choose 4):** Eidetic Memory, Loyal Companion (Scryfish), Night Vision, Orientation, Witch-Sight, **any Miracles of The Ethersea**
- **Equipment:** Mantle and robes (Light Armour); finger claws; dowerchime staff (Quarterstaff); ethersea cloak; cyfar compass (Holy Symbol); a waterskin of seawater; **230 drops** Aqua Ghyranis.

## Isharann Tidecaster

- **Species:** Aelf (Idoneth Deepkin)
- **Attributes:** Body 1 · Mind 4 · Soul 2
- **Skills (7 XP):** Arcana, Beast Handling, **Channelling (Core)**, Determination, Devotion, Intuition, Lore, Nature, Reflexes, Survival
- **Core Talents:** Spellcasting (The Deeps), Unbind
- **Talents (Choose 3):** Arcane Discipline, Iron Will\*, Loyal Companion (Spirit Guardian), Scholar, Unbreakable Spells, Witch-Sight
- **Equipment:** Azure scales (Light Armour); pelagic staff (Quarterstaff); ethersea cloak; a carved Aelven rune meaning "water"; two watertight scroll cases; **200 drops** Aqua Ghyranis.

### Rules-relevant notes
- Soulscryer's Blessed (The Ethersea) grants Miracles of The Ethersea (see `08-miracles/`).
- Idoneth Species: Aelf (Long-Lived bonus applies: +2 Skill XP, same Skill; see `03-species/`).
