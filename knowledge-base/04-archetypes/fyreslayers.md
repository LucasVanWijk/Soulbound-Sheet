# Fyreslayers Archetypes

Source: Corebook pp. 57–60. Faction page reference: Fyreslayers (p. 192). Species: **Duardin (Fyreslayers)**.

Fyreslayers **eschew armour** and rely on ur-gold runes (see `09-equipment/`, ur-gold runes at p. 117). Freeform: choose a forename and a **lodge name** only.

> Lore is omitted. Only the rules-relevant stat blocks are kept.

---

## Auric Runesmiter

- **Species:** Duardin (Fyreslayers)
- **Attributes:** Body 3 · Mind 2 · Soul 3
- **Skills (7 XP):** Ballistic Skill, Beast Handling, Crafting, Determination, **Devotion (Core)**, Fortitude, Intuition, Might, Theology, Weapon Skill
- **Core Talents:** Blessed (Grimnir), Sense Ur-Gold, Zharrgrim
- **Talents (Choose 2):** Diplomat, Fearless, Guts\*, **any Miracles of Grimnir**
- **Equipment:** Any three ur-gold runes (p. 117); a runic iron and handaxe **or** a latch-axe (Greataxe); a Forge Key (Holy Symbol); enough ur-gold to forge one ur-gold rune; **15 drops** Aqua Ghyranis.

## Battlesmith

- **Species:** Duardin (Fyreslayers)
- **Attributes:** Body 2 · Mind 2 · Soul 3
- **Skills (12 XP):** Arcana, Athletics, Awareness, Ballistic Skill, Crafting, Determination, Devotion, **Entertain (Core)**, Fortitude, Guile, Intuition, Lore, Reflexes, Theology, Weapon Skill
- **Core Talent:** Legendary Saga
- **Talents (Choose 4):** Blessed (Grimnir)\*, Bulwark, Eidetic Memory, Forbidden Knowledge, Guts\*, Observant, Scholar
- **Equipment:** Rune of Iron Skin; Rune of Relentless Zeal; any one ur-gold rune (p. 117); ancestral battleaxe; handaxe; icon of Grimnir; a palm-sized nugget of ur-gold; **95 drops** Aqua Ghyranis.

## Doomseeker

- **Species:** Duardin (Fyreslayers)
- **Attributes:** Body 4 · Mind 1 · Soul 2
- **Skills (9 XP):** Athletics, Awareness, Ballistic Skill, Determination, Fortitude, Intimidation, Intuition, Might, Reflexes, Survival, Theology, **Weapon Skill (Core)**
- **Core Talent:** Barazakdum, the Doom-oath
- **Talents (Choose 3):** Battle Rage, Guts\*, Hard to Kill, Intimidating Manner, Iron Stomach, Underdog
- **Equipment:** Rune of Iron Skin; any one ur-gold rune (p. 117); any Common two-handed melee weapon **or** any two Common one-handed melee weapons; a memento from home; a bedroll; a few shavings of ur-gold; **110 drops** Aqua Ghyranis.

### Rules-relevant notes
- Fyreslayer Species Bonus: Too Stubborn to Die (+2 max Wounds, see `03-species/`).
- Fyreslayer "Soulbound without pay" is roleplay guidance only; no mechanical effect.
- The Zharrgrim/Blessed (Grimnir) pathway grants Miracles of Grimnir (see `08-miracles/`).
