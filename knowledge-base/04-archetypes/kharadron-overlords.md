# Kharadron Overlords Archetypes

Source: Corebook pp. 65–68. Faction page reference: Kharadron Overlords (p. 194). Species: **Duardin (Kharadron Overlords)**.

Kharadron choose **one of the sample rigs** instead of freeform equipment (rigs listed on p. 116; see `09-equipment/`).

> Lore is omitted. Only the rules-relevant stat blocks are kept.

---

## Aether-Khemist

- **Species:** Duardin (Kharadron Overlords)
- **Attributes:** Body 2 · Mind 4 · Soul 1
- **Skills (9 XP):** Awareness, Ballistic Skill, **Crafting (Core)**, Dexterity, Fortitude, Guile, Lore, Medicine, Nature, Reflexes, Survival, Weapon Skill
- **Core Talent:** Aether-Khemists Guild Member
- **Talents (Choose 3):** Acute Sense, Forbidden Knowledge, Iron Stomach, Medic\*, Observant, Scholar
- **Equipment:** Aether-Khemist's Rig (p. 116); a Khemist's Kit; a book of notes and formulae; **100D worth** of aethercrafting materials (book prints "100D"; treat as Kharadron currency value, see `09-equipment/`); **85 drops** Aqua Ghyranis.

## Endrinmaster

- **Species:** Duardin (Kharadron Overlords)
- **Attributes:** Body 2 · Mind 3 · Soul 2
- **Skills (10 XP):** Athletics, Awareness, Ballistic Skill, **Crafting (Core)**, Determination, Dexterity, Fortitude, Guile, Intuition, Might, Reflexes, Survival, Weapon Skill
- **Core Talent:** Endrineers Guild Member
- **Talents (Choose 5):** Combat Ready, Combat Repairs, Eidetic Memory, Iron Will\*, Savvy, Scholar, Sleight of Hand, Tactician
- **Equipment:** Endrinmaster's Rig (p. 116); Endrineer's Tools; a selection of sketches for experimental devices; **45 drops** Aqua Ghyranis.

## Skyrigger

- **Species:** Duardin (Kharadron Overlords)
- **Attributes:** Body 3 · Mind 3 · Soul 1
- **Core Skill:** **Ballistic Skill OR Crafting** (player chooses one; receives Training 1 + Focus 1 in it)
- **Skills (9 XP):** Athletics, Awareness, Ballistic Skill, Crafting, Determination, Dexterity, Fortitude, Guile, Intuition, Reflexes, Survival, Weapon Skill
- **Core Talent:** **Endrineers Guild Member OR Hit and Run** (player chooses one)
- **Talents (Choose 4):** Combat Repairs\*, Demolitions Expert\*, Endrineers Guild Member\*, Fearless, Hit and Run, Quick Reload, Savvy
- **Equipment:** Endrinrigger's Rig **or** Skywarden's Rig (p. 116); any Common melee weapon; two aetheric grenades **or** skymines; a lucky charm; **25 drops** Aqua Ghyranis.

---

### Rules-relevant notes
- Kharadron immunity to **aether-gold madness** is described as a flavour benefit of Soulbound status. No separate Condition rule is given in these archetypes; treat as narrative unless a Talent/Condition states otherwise.
- Skyrigger's "Ballistic or Crafting" and "Endrineers Guild Member or Hit and Run" are **choices** and validation must enforce exactly one of each.
