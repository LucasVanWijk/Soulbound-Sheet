# Archetypes

Source: Corebook Chapter 3 (pp. 44–78). The core book contains **23 Archetypes** across **7 factions**.

An Archetype is the character's starting identity. It defines:
- Starting **Attributes** (Body / Mind / Soul)
- **Species** (fixed, or a choice)
- **Core Skill** (starts at Training 1 + Focus 1)
- **Skills** to choose from, with a **Skill XP** budget
- **Core Talent(s)** (granted automatically) and **Talents** to choose (a number is given, "Choose N")
- **Starting equipment**
- **Aqua Ghyranis** (drops of currency)

## Format of each Archetype entry (book p. 45)

- Name, one-line summary, description, quote
- **Attributes** (B / M / S start values; may swap two per Step 2)
- **Species** (and any faction note, used for Talent and Equipment requirements)
- **Skills** (XP to spend; Core Skill starts at Training 1 + Focus 1)
- **Talents** (Core Talent(s) given; "Choose N" from a short list; `*` = requirement outside the Core Skill)
- **Equipment** (starting gear; Aqua Ghyranis drops)

## Global Archetype Rules (book pp. 45–46)

- You may swap any two starting Attributes (see `02-character-creation/attributes.md`).
- Core Skill starts at **Training 1 + Focus 1**.
- Spend the listed Skill XP on new Skills or improve the Core Skill. Costs per level are in `11-advancement/README.md`.
- Core Talents are granted automatically. Other Talents are picked from the list; a Talent marked with an asterisk (*) has a requirement outside the Core Skill (for example, Iron Will needs Training (1) in Determination).
- Talents that need equipment or tools (e.g. *A Warm Meal*, *Medic*) grant that equipment automatically during creation. After creation, you must buy it.
- Starting equipment: at least one weapon or means of defence; may also start with armour, supplies, trinkets or heirlooms. All Archetypes start with wealth (Aqua Ghyranis).
- Custom Archetypes: see p. 296 (GM discretion) or freeform creation (p. 295).
- **Gendered names** of Archetypes (e.g. Darkling Sorceress, Excelsior Warpriest) do not restrict who may play them.
