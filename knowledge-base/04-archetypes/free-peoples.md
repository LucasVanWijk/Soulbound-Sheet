# Free Peoples Archetypes

Source: Corebook pp. 47–53. The Free Peoples are the descendants of those who fled to Azyr. Their faction note is "Free Peoples" (see `README.md`).

Factions within the Free Peoples: Collegiate Arcane (p. 187), Darkling Covens (p. 188), Devoted of Sigmar (p. 190), Scourge Privateers (p. 195).

---

## 1. Battlemage

- **Summary:** A wizard of the Collegiate Arcane, trained to harness the magic of the realms.
- **Species:** Human
- **Attributes:** Body 1 · Mind 4 · Soul 2
- **Skills (9 XP):** Arcana, Awareness, Channelling (Core), Crafting, Determination, Dexterity, Guile, Intuition, Lore, Medicine, Reflexes, Theology
- **Core Talents:** Spellcasting (choose), Unbind
- **Talents (Choose 2):** Forbidden Knowledge, Iron Will\*, Loyal Companion (choose), Unbreakable Spells, Witch-Sight
- **Equipment:** Wizard's staff (Quarterstaff); Battlemage robes of your Lore (Light Armour); an arcane focus; a grimoire; an Aqshian coal; ink and quill; chalk; a number of small pouches with various oddities; **185 drops** Aqua Ghyranis.

## 2. Black Ark Corsair

- **Summary:** With salt water in your veins, you cross the hostile seas of the Mortal Realms in search of fortune.
- **Species:** Aelf
- **Attributes:** Body 3 · Mind 2 · Soul 2
- **Skills (12 XP):** Athletics, Awareness, Ballistic Skill, Beast Handling, Dexterity, Fortitude, Guile, Intimidation, Intuition, Medicine, Nature, Reflexes, Survival, Stealth, **Weapon Skill (Core)**
- **Core Talent:** The Bigger They Are
- **Talents (Choose 4):** Ambidextrous, Animal Friend, Fearless, Hunter, Opportunist, Relentless Assault, Silver Tongue
- **Equipment:** Corsair armour (Medium Armour); a repeater handbow and cutlass (Sword) **or** two cutlasses; Sea-dragon cloak; a waterskin filled with strong alcohol; a trophy from a beast you've slain; **45 drops** Aqua Ghyranis.

## 3. Darkling Sorceress

- **Summary:** A master of enchantment and illusion; everyone else is a tool to satisfy your whim.
- **Species:** Aelf
- **Attributes:** Body 1 · Mind 3 · Soul 3
- **Skills (9 XP):** Awareness, Arcana, **Channelling (Core)**, Devotion, Guile, Intimidation, Intuition, Lore, Theology, Reflexes, Stealth, Weapon Skill
- **Core Talents:** Spellcasting (**Grey**), Unbind
- **Talents (Choose 3):** Alley Cat, Criminal, Master of Disguise, Silver Tongued, Vanish\*, Witch-Sight
- **Equipment:** Dark Leather (Light Armour); witchstaff (Quarterstaff); ebon dagger; a small bottle of a favoured scent; a disguise kit; two phials of basic poison; a parchment listing names with drops of blood; **220 drops** Aqua Ghyranis.

## 4. Excelsior Warpriest

- **Summary:** A warrior-priest of the God-King, willing to do anything in the name of your divine liege.
- **Species:** Human
- **Attributes:** Body 2 · Mind 1 · Soul 4
- **Skills (7 XP):** Determination, **Devotion (Core)**, Fortitude, Intimidation, Lore, Medicine, Might, Reflexes, Theology, Weapon Skill
- **Core Talent:** Blessed (Sigmar)
- **Talents (Choose 4):** Intimidating Manner, Iron Will\*, Loyal Companion (Gryph-hound), Medic\*, Sigmar's Judgement\*, **any Miracles of Sigmar**
- **Equipment:** Warpriest's vestments (Light Armour); a warhammer; a hand hammer; a Sigmarite Tome (Holy Symbol); a cloak; a bedroll; an apothecary's satchel; a storm lantern; a pipette; **160 drops** Aqua Ghyranis.

## 5. Trade Pioneer

- **Summary:** A natural adventurer, you brave the Mortal Realms in search of riches, glory, and knowledge.
- **Species:** **Any** (player's choice)
- **Attributes:** Body 2 · Mind 3 · Soul 2
- **Skills (12 XP):** **Any** (player's choice)
- **Core Skill:** Survival
- **Core Talent:** Trader's Cache
- **Talents (Choose 4):** A Warm Meal, Diplomat, Forbidden Knowledge, Loyal Companion (Mustori), Savvy, Scholar, Witch-Sight
- **Equipment:** Traveller's gear (Light Armour); any Common melee weapon; any Common ranged weapon; **one piece of Rare equipment**; a tent; a blanket; 5 days' rations; a backpack; **55 drops** Aqua Ghyranis.

---

### Notes for the app

- Battlemage: Spellcasting is a Core Talent with a "choose" tradition (see `06-talents/` and `07-spells/`).
- Darkling Sorceress's Spellcasting is restricted to the **Grey** tradition in the book.
- Excelsior Warpriest's "any Miracles of Sigmar" is an allowed Talent choice (i.e. Blessed grants Miracles).
- Trade Pioneer's Species and Skill choices are **open** (validation must allow any Species and any Skill).
