# Stormcast Eternals Archetypes

Source: Corebook pp. 69–73. Faction page reference: Stormcast Eternals (p. 196). Species: **Stormcast Eternal**.

**Restrictions (from Species, see `03-species/`):**
- A Stormcast Eternal **cannot become Soulbound**, and **cannot use Soulfire**.
- When computing party Soulfire, **Stormcast Eternal characters are excluded** from the count (see `01-core-rules/soulfire-doom.md`).
- Species Bonus: **Reforged** (roll 1d6 for Reforge count at creation; 6 = roll again and add).
- Stormcast armour is **Sigmarite** (Sigmarite Trait applies to equipment in freeform).

> Lore is omitted. Only the rules-relevant stat blocks are kept.

---

## Knight-Azyros

- **Species:** Stormcast Eternal
- **Attributes:** Body 3 · Mind 2 · Soul 4
- **Skills (7 XP):** Athletics, Awareness, Determination, **Devotion (Core)**, Intimidation, Intuition, Medicine, Reflexes, Theology, Weapon Skill
- **Core Talent:** Blessed (Sigmar)
- **Talents (Choose 3):** Diplomat, Iron Will\*, Medic\*, Scholar, **any Miracles of Sigmar**
- **Equipment:** Sigmarite plate (Medium Armour); starblade (Sword); dagger; celestial wings; celestial beacon (Holy Symbol); **85 drops** Aqua Ghyranis.

## Knight-Incantor

- **Species:** Stormcast Eternal
- **Attributes:** Body 3 · Mind 4 · Soul 2
- **Skills (7 XP):** Arcana, Beast Handling, **Channelling (Core)**, Crafting, Determination, Intuition, Lore, Medicine, Theology, Weapon Skill
- **Core Talents:** Spellcasting (**Celestial**), Unbind
- **Talents (Choose 2):** Forbidden Knowledge, Iron Will\*, Potent Spells, Scholar, Witch-Sight
- **Equipment:** Sigmarite plate (Medium Armour); incantor's staff (Quarterstaff); dagger; tabard of the Sacrosanct Chamber; grimoire; scroll case; **240 drops** Aqua Ghyranis.

## Knight-Questor

- **Species:** Stormcast Eternal
- **Attributes:** Body 4 · Mind 2 · Soul 3
- **Skills (7 XP):** Athletics, Determination, Fortitude, Intimidation, Lore, Might, Reflexes, Survival, Theology, **Weapon Skill (Core)**
- **Core Talent:** Heroic Challenge
- **Talents (Choose 3):** Bulwark, Combat Ready, Diplomat, Guts\*, Intimidating Manner, Underdog
- **Equipment:** Sigmarite warplate (Heavy Armour); **questor warblade (Sword) and shield, or any two-handed melee weapon**; a cloak; a small pendant bearing the mark of your previous Stormhost; **65 drops** Aqua Ghyranis.

## Knight-Venator

- **Species:** Stormcast Eternal
- **Attributes:** Body 3 · Mind 3 · Soul 3
- **Skills (9 XP):** Athletics, Awareness, **Ballistic Skill (Core)**, Beast Handling, Determination, Dexterity, Guile, Nature, Reflexes, Stealth, Survival, Weapon Skill
- **Core Talent:** Loyal Companion (Star-eagle)
- **Talents (Choose 3):** Combat Ready, Fearless, Hunter, Orientation, Star-fated Arrow, Vanish
- **Equipment:** Sigmarite plate (Medium Armour); realmhunter's bow (Bow); a quiver and arrows; storm gladius (Sword); celestial wings; **90 drops** Aqua Ghyranis.

### Rules-relevant notes
- Knight-Incantor's Spellcasting is the **Celestial** tradition (not Grey, Jade, etc.). See `07-spells/`.
- Knight-Questor's Core Talent is **Heroic Challenge** (no requirement noted in the stat block; see `06-talents/`).
- Knight-Venator's Core Talent is a **Loyal Companion** (Star-eagle); its specific companion stats are on p. 73 and in the Loyal Companion Talent entry (`06-talents/`).
