# Daughters of Khaine Archetypes

Source: Corebook pp. 54–56. Faction page reference: Daughters of Khaine (p. 189). Species: **Aelf (Daughters of Khaine)**.

> Lore is omitted. Only the rules-relevant stat blocks are kept.

---

## Hag Priestess

- **Species:** Aelf (Daughters of Khaine)
- **Attributes:** Body 2 · Mind 2 · Soul 4
- **Skills (7 XP):** Awareness, Determination, **Devotion (Core)**, Dexterity, Guile, Intuition, Lore, Medicine, Reflexes, Stealth, Theology, Weapon Skill
- **Core Talent:** Blessed (Khaine)
- **Talents (Choose 3):** Backstab\*, Fearless, Forbidden Knowledge, Night Vision, **any Miracles of Khaine**
- **Equipment:** Ceremonial armour (Light Armour); sacrificial blade (Dagger); bloodstained ritual chalice (Holy Symbol); a whetstone; manacles; pestle and mortar; **280 drops** Aqua Ghyranis.

## Witch Aelf

- **Species:** Aelf (Daughters of Khaine)
- **Attributes:** Body 3 · Mind 1 · Soul 3
- **Skills (9 XP):** Athletics, Awareness, Ballistic Skill, Devotion, Dexterity, Fortitude, Guile, Intimidation, Reflexes, Stealth, Theology, **Weapon Skill (Core)**
- **Core Talent:** Blood Frenzy
- **Talents (Choose 4):** Ambidextrous, Backstab\*, Combat Ready, Contortionist, Graceful Landing, Relentless Assault, Vanish\*
- **Equipment:** Leather armour (Light Armour); two sacrificial blades (2 × Dagger); three phials of basic poison; a bottle of acid; **220 drops** Aqua Ghyranis.

---

### Rules-relevant notes
- Soulbound Daughters of Khaine lose formal connection to the temple. No mechanical effect in the book.
- Blessed (Khaine) grants access to Miracles of Khaine (see `08-miracles/`).
